# Support KB — Password Reset FAQ

## How do I reset my password?

If you forgot your password for the Reporting System:

1. Go to the login page at `https://reporting.example.com/login`
2. Click **Forgot Password**
3. Enter your corporate email address
4. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 24 hours)
5. Create a new password meeting complexity requirements (12+ chars, upper, lower, number, symbol)

## Password reset link expired

If your reset link expired, request a new one from the login page. Links expire after 24 hours for security.

## Still cannot log in after reset

If you still receive "Unauthorized" after resetting:
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try an incognito/private window
- Verify you are using Production URL, not Staging
- Contact support if the issue persists — your account may need re-provisioning
